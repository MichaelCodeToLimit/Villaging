# FAQ and troubleshooting

**Nothing happens when I talk to a villager.** Open `/villaging key` and check your provider and key. A busy AI service makes the villager keep trying; it tells you it is working on it.

**A villager will not do something.** Say what you want in other words, or ask what it can do. Risky acts need your yes in the pop-up.

**Does it work on a server?** Yes. Install it on the server and on every player's client.

**Does it work with vanilla clients?** Not yet. A server plugin is planned for later.

**Where do I report bugs?** Open an issue with your `logs/latest.log` (check no key appears in it).
